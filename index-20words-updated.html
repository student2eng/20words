Frequently asked questions

Learning with 20words.online

Learn more about the three learning stages, supported levels, and our free learning approach.

Is 20words.online free?

Yes. All learning features are free. Contributions are completely optional and help support the website and its continued development.

How does the learning system work?

The learning journey has three stages: Start Learning, Missing Letters, and Learn with Examples.

What happens in Start Learning?

You study new words and revisit words you have not mastered. The session can also include mastered words that are due for review.

What is Missing Letters?

It is a spelling activity where you complete a word by entering one missing letter in each box.

What happens in Learn with Examples?

You see a previously studied word in an example sentence and answer a question about its meaning in context.

Which English levels are available?

The platform supports A1 Beginner, A2 Elementary, B1 Intermediate, B2 Upper Intermediate, and C1 Advanced.

Do I need to pay or subscribe?

No subscription or payment is required to use the learning features. Optional contributions are a way to support the platform.
